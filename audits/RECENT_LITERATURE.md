## Most recent numerical construction

[Zhang–Zhu, arXiv:2605.00590v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00590v2), revised 11 June 2026, is a **public preprint**. Theorem 1.1 supplies `[12,12,18]` over characteristic-not-two fields containing a square root of -1. Section 1.2 states the `[11,11,18]` restriction. Two separate agents checked all 20,736 coefficient equations, and the parent reran the retained exact Gaussian-integer verifier. No complex exactness claim follows from the construction alone.

The current arXiv history was checked for later revisions. No later primary small-cell improvement was identified in the searches below. Publication or indexing delays remain possible.

## Hrubeš and 2025–2026 citation leads

[Hrubeš, CCC 2024](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CCC.2024.12) proves a complex subquadratic asymptotic upper bound. Its Theorem 9 supplies an explicit finite construction; those parameters were inspected rather than extracting a numerical value from big-O notation.

The journal successor, [SIAM Journal on Computing 55(2), 451–467 (2026)](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/24M1666884), appeared online on 30 April 2026. Its available author manuscript is dated 5 June 2024. These dates refer to different publication stages. No competitive small diagonal specialization was established in this audit.

[Rossman–Zhu, ITCS 2026](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol362-itcs2026/html/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.113/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.113.html), also circulated as arXiv:2512.01227, was followed as a recent citation lead. It concerns complexity-theoretic consequences of lower bounds and does not give an improved individual Hurwitz cell used here.

[Lynn's journal paper and public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.05369) were inspected for characteristic and algebraic-closure results. They supply field policy, not a new small-cell table. Searches for subsequent algebraically closed-field work did not identify another accepted numerical improvement.

## Later integer construction families

[Lenzhen–Morier-Genoud–Ovsienko (2011)](https://sophie-moriergenoud.perso.math.cnrs.fr/Publi/HurwitzPbFin.pdf), Theorems 1–2, and [Hu–Huang–Zhang (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01502), Theorems 3–7, Corollary 5.4, Proposition 5.7, and stated exceptions, were inspected. Integer constructions would give valid complex upper bounds.

The retained [finite comparison script](../scripts/check_later_families.py) enumerates 19,607 instances for the family parameter `n=4..32`, including symmetry and ordinary source restrictions. None strictly improves any of the 248 entries in Shapiro p.292. This comparison does not cover all possible combinations of construction methods or establish that larger parameters can never help.

[Zhang–Huang (2017)](https://sigma-journal.com/2017/064/sigma17-064.pdf) gives an extended doubling construction. Shapiro's [subsequent construction note](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/u.osu.edu/dist/c/5162/files/2018/06/Constructions-1jdsyms.pdf) was also read. These do not license an unrestricted tensor-product operation on arbitrary composition formulas. Such an operation is absent from the ledger builder.

## Backward citation updates

The 2011/2018 references led back to Yiu and Sánchez-Flores (1996). The [official 1996 issue booklet](https://smm.org.mx/boletin_anterior/v2/n2.pdf) is a collection of article first pages, not full papers. Yiu's abstract announces improvements over Smith–Yiu. The parent then inspected Shapiro's Chapter 13 Appendix C, whose p.291 explicitly identifies the sources consolidated in the p.292 table. That published table supersedes reliance on the 1992 table alone. The original full proofs remain an access gap.

The lower-bound citation chain led to Dugger–Isaksen's corrected BP2 criterion and Antoniano–Gitler's axial-map obstruction. Both were inspected and independently reviewed. The inaccessible original Lam–Randall table remains a possible source of stronger lower bounds; no unpublished or unverified values were substituted.
