# Known gaps

This release freezes supported bounds. It does not certify a globally exhaustive best-known frontier.

1. **Original Lam–Randall table.** The 1995 proceedings source contains section-count information beyond the part reprinted by Shapiro. Full text was not obtained. In particular, stronger cellwise lower bounds for inputs 18–32 may be available there or in later immersion/section literature. Shapiro's inspected block through 17 is not extrapolated.
2. **Original 1996 construction proofs.** Complete Yiu and Sánchez-Flores articles were not located. The official issue booklet contains only article first pages. The numerical authority used here is Shapiro's published consolidation, p.292. The original citation chain should still be completed.
3. **Historical topology.** Adams's theorem and Shapiro's presentation of the Adams/Atiyah K-theory consequences were inspected, along with Xie's stronger characteristic-independent statement. The original Atiyah paper was not obtained in full. No numerical row relies solely on an uninspected historical attribution.
4. **BP text version.** Numerical consequences use the inspected current author manuscript. Comparison against the final typeset journal PDF remains desirable; the earlier arXiv text is explicitly distinguished.
5. **Search coverage.** Concept-based web, arXiv, author-page, publisher, and citation-chain searches ran through 26 September 2026. Indexing delays, inaccessible papers, and missed terminology can conceal improvements. No search proves absence of later literature.
6. **Finite construction comparison.** The 2011/2018 families were checked over the recorded finite parameter range against the classical table. The builder closes its accepted seed bounds under restriction and one-input direct sums within the window. It does not exhaust every doubling, tensor construction with extra hypotheses, or combination of all published constructions. Sources outside the window could also restrict to improve a cell inside it.
7. **Scope.** The canonical window is `1 <= r <= s <= 32`. Larger public tables were located but are not transcribed or claimed audited in this release. Other fields require separate ledgers.
8. **External review.** The independent checks were performed by separate AI agents, then adjudicated by the parent auditor. This is not external human peer review. Researchers are invited to inspect and correct the data.

Within a bounded row, the true value lies in the recorded bracket if its cited results hold. “Open cells” means unequal audited bounds, not a global assertion that no exact value has been published. No unresolved source conflict is encoded as an exact value.
