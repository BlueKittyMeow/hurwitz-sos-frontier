## smith-yiu-1992

Tara L. Smith, Paul Yiu. Constructions of sums of squares formulae with integer coefficients. Boletín de la Sociedad Matemática Mexicana (2) 37, 479–495 (1992).

**Status:** peer-reviewed journal. **Role:** numerical authority. **Checked:** 2026-09-26.

Public access: [source 1](https://boletin.math.org.mx/pdf/2/37/BSMM%282%29.37.479-495.pdf).

Locators: Theorem (1), p.479; Theorem (2), p.480 (historical comparison); Proposition (7), p.484; Theorem (9), p.485; Sections 7–9: constructions.

- Integer-coefficient existence transfers to C by scalar extension. Integer optimality does not.
- Small-input theorem covers r<=9 or s<=9; r o s is the least target satisfying the Hopf parity condition.
